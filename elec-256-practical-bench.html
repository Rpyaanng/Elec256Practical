ELEC-256 midterm lab · Part B practical

Wire it, meter it, get it stamped.

A bench simulator for the series-parallel practical: banana leads, a trainer board, a DC supply, a bench ammeter and a handheld DMM. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, WASD to move, click jacks to plug in.

The circuit: R1 = 750 Ω in series with R2 = 3000 Ω, then R3 = 1.5 kΩ in parallel with R4 = 1.5 kΩ. Source = 30 V. One ammeter, measuring the current through R3.
